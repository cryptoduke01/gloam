Owner guard handover

Hands each vault to VaultOwnerGuard, which forwards only deposit limit and fee changes and the sweep of stray native coin. After step 2 on a chain, nobody can replace that vault's proof checkers or move its ownership, ever, this wallet included. There is no undo. Run it from the runbook in DEPLOY.md, smallest vault first. Connect 0x76b7…D4D8. Neither step moves funds.

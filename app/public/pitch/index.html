The idea

A payment has one owner.
So nobody else needs a key.

Shared secrets

Auctions, order books, lending

Someone must compute on many people's hidden data. FHE and MPC networks like Zama and Arcium use a committee that holds the keys.

Payments

One owner

The owner proves the payment on their own device with zero-knowledge proofs. Nobody else ever holds a key.

Breach a committee and everyone is exposed, past payments too. Breach one device and one person is.

Say: Here's the idea. Privacy splits into two jobs. Things like sealed auctions and lending pools need someone to compute on lots of people's hidden data, so FHE and MPC networks like Zama and Arcium use a committee that holds the keys. A payment is different: it has one owner. The owner can prove it on their own device, and nobody else ever needs a key. That matters when something gets hacked. Breach a committee and everyone is exposed, including past payments. Breach one phone and it's one person. We don't invent new cryptography. We use the proven maths from the Zcash lineage and build the payments product on top, like Stripe built on card networks.

How it works

One private balance you can
pay from and prove from.

Add money

Stablecoins, ETH or Robinhood Chain stock tokens go into the vault. Your balance becomes private.

Pay privately

Send, run payroll or let an agent pay. Amounts and people stay hidden.

Prove what's needed

Show a bank, landlord or accountant one fact, and nothing else.

Proofs are made on your device, never on our servers.

Say: Gloam is a self-custodial vault. You put money in and get a private balance only you can see. From there you pay people, run payroll, or let an agent pay, without showing amounts or who got paid. When someone needs to know something, like an accountant needing a payroll total, you prove exactly that and nothing more. Every proof is made on your device, never on our servers. You can cash out anytime.

What's new

Not new cryptography.
A new building block.

Payroll total proof

Proves a run of up to 32 payments paid exactly a total, without showing who got what.

Proofs for one reader

Who it is for and when it expires are sealed into the proof. Anyone checks it in a browser, no wallet.

Private agent payments

A private method proposed for MPP, private x402, and an MCP server with spending limits.

Private payments you can prove. As far as our research found, these three are new.

Not new: the Zcash-lineage private pool and viewing keys. We build on them.11
Say: So what's actually new? Not the cryptography. Three things on top of it. First, a payroll total proof: it proves a run of up to 32 payments added up to exactly a total, without showing who got what. We found nothing like it. Second, proofs for one reader: who it's for and when it expires are sealed into the proof, change either and it fails, and anyone can check it in a browser with no wallet. Viewing keys elsewhere show your whole history forever. Third, private payments for agents: we proposed a private payment method for MPP, the machine payments standard from Tempo and Stripe, which had none, plus private x402 and an MCP server with spending limits.

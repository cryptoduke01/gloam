The problem

Every wallet is a
public bank statement.

Payroll

Pay a team onchain and every salary is public, forever.

Treasury

Balances, suppliers and runway are readable by anyone.

Trading

Every position and trade size is visible, so others copy or front-run you.

AI agents

An agent's wallet shows every tool it pays for, and its strategy.

Say: Paste any wallet into a block explorer and you see everything: the balance, every payment, every trade, who it paid and how much. That's fine for settlement, and terrible for real money. It's why companies keep payroll and treasury off chain, why traders get copied and front-run, and why agents that pay for tools leak what they're doing.

The solution

One private balance you can
pay from and prove from.

Add money

Stablecoins, crypto or tokenized stocks go into the vault. Your balance becomes private.

Pay privately

Send, run payroll or let an agent pay. Amounts and people stay hidden.

Prove what's needed

Show a bank, landlord or accountant one fact, and nothing else.

Say: Gloam is a self-custodial vault. You put money in and get a private balance only you can see. From there you pay people, run payroll, or let an agent pay, without showing amounts or who got paid. And when someone needs to know something, like an accountant needing a payroll total, you prove exactly that and nothing more. You can cash out anytime.

Stablecoin issuer rules respected on Tempo

Say: The first question we always get: isn't this a mixer? No. Every deposit and cash out is screened against sanctions lists. On Tempo we respect the stablecoin issuer's own rules. Users can prove things to auditors and banks. And we removed ourselves: no admin can move anyone's money, and any rule change waits three days in public.

Pays for APIs over x402 and MPP
Spending caps per payment and per day
Private method proposed to the MPP spec

Say: Agents are where payments are going. With Gloam an agent pays for APIs privately, over x402 or MPP, inside spending limits its owner sets. We proposed a private payment method to MPP, the machine payments standard from Tempo and Stripe; the pull request is open. And anyone can add Gloam to Claude or ChatGPT by pasting one link.
